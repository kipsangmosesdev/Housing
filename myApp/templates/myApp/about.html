About XbomaSwift

Where home meets heart. Bringing trust, technology, and Kenyan warmth into every move you make.

Our Story

Every Kenyan knows the struggle of finding a decent home. Walking under the hot sun, chasing “TO LET” signs, dealing with brokers, and facing hidden costs can be exhausting.

XbomaSwift was born from that frustration and a dream to make things better. We imagined a Kenya where finding a house is simple, affordable, and honest—where technology works hand in hand with trust.

Our journey began with one goal: to make renting easier. Along the way, we realized that moving is part of the same story. So, we created a space where you can find your home and your mover—all in one trusted platform.

We do not own moving trucks. Instead, we empower hardworking movers who are already part of our community to connect directly with tenants who need them. It’s convenience built on collaboration, the Kenyan way.

Our Mission

To empower landlords, tenants, and movers across Kenya with a trusted, technology-driven platform that simplifies renting and relocation.

Our Vision

To be Kenya’s leading digital home for renting and moving, where every Kenyan can find a safe, affordable home and trusted movers with ease.

Moving Services

Moving should be exciting, not stressful. After you find your next home on XbomaSwift, we help you take the next step with ease.

Our verified movers are part of a growing network built on reliability and care. Whether you’re moving across town or to a new county, we make sure your journey is smooth and safe.

We don’t just move your belongings—we move your experience from stressful to seamless. That’s what makes XbomaSwift different.

Reviews and Ratings

Trust is earned, not claimed. That’s why XbomaSwift is built on openness and real feedback.

System Reviews show what our users love about their experience—from the simplicity of our search tools to the accuracy of our maps.

Property Reviews reveal what living in a home is truly like, shared by real tenants. You’ll know about safety, cleanliness, and accessibility before you even visit.

We believe honest voices build strong communities. Every review on XbomaSwift strengthens that trust.
