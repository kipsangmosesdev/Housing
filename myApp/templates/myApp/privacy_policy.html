Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2, 2025
Last Updated: October 26, 2025

XbomaSwift ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data while you interact with our platform, www.xbomaswift.com.

By using XbomaSwift, you agree to the practices described in this policy and acknowledge that all activities on the platform are securely recorded to enhance accountability, support tax reporting, and assist in dispute resolution. This policy applies to all users — Tenants, Landlords, and Movers.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

  • Full name and contact details
  • Email address and phone number (for login, verification, and M-Pesa payments)
  • User type (Tenant, Landlord, or Mover)
  • Location and profile preferences

b. Property Information (Landlords)

  • Property title, description, and location
  • Images, rental prices, and amenities
  • Availability status, views, and ratings

c. Mover Service Information (Movers)

  • Business or individual mover name and contact details
  • Service descriptions, pricing, and operating areas
  • Customer reviews and service ratings
  • Business permit or certification details (where applicable)

d. Usage Data

  • Pages, properties, and mover services viewed
  • Search filters, map interactions, and contact requests
  • Favorite listings (Wishlist) and viewing history for registered users

e. Payment and Swift Transaction Data

  • M-Pesa phone number and payment confirmation
  • Transaction history, Swifts purchased, and balance
  • Subscription and promotion payments for landlords and movers
  • Transaction ID, timestamps, and status (no sensitive card data stored)
  • All transactions are recorded for accuracy, tax filing, and dispute resolution

f. Device and Technical Data

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, OS, and device details
  • Session timestamps and referral sources

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To manage and verify user accounts for tenants, landlords, and movers
  • To process Swift purchases, subscription fees, and mover service payments
  • To recommend nearby properties and verified moving services
  • To enhance platform performance and reliability using automation
  • To verify property listings, service quality, and transaction integrity
  • To improve trust, accountability, and user experience across all services

3. How We Share Your Information

  • With verified tenants, landlords, or movers after Swift-based access or approval
  • With third-party service providers such as M-Pesa for secure payments
  • With government or tax agencies when required by Kenyan law
  • During legitimate audits, investigations, or dispute resolutions
  • We do not sell or rent user data to any third party

4. Data Retention

Your data is securely stored as long as your account remains active or as required by law, business needs, or taxation rules in Kenya. Inactive accounts are archived and eventually deleted following regulatory compliance standards.

5. Cookies and Tracking

XbomaSwift does not use cookies. Instead, registered users manage activity through personalized features such as:

  • Wishlist — saves favorite properties or mover services
  • View history — allows users to revisit previous listings

This approach gives users control of their data while maintaining privacy and transparency.

6. Location Data

  • Used to show nearby properties and mover services
  • Data is temporary and not permanently stored

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • View or clear your property and mover history
  • Withdraw consent for updates or marketing communication
  • Disable location sharing via your browser or device settings

8. Data Security

We employ strong security measures supported by AI-driven monitoring to ensure data integrity and prevent unauthorized access. Measures include:

  • End-to-end SSL encryption across all user sessions
  • Hashed passwords and secure authentication methods
  • Automated transaction and service activity logging
  • Restricted admin-level data access and periodic system reviews
  • Regular updates to maintain compliance with Kenya’s Data Protection Act

9. Consent

By creating an account or using XbomaSwift, you consent to the collection, use, and processing of your personal data as outlined in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

10. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach, XbomaSwift will promptly notify affected users and report the incident to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) as required under the Data Protection Act, 2019.

11. Cross-Border Data Transfers

If personal data is processed or stored outside Kenya, we ensure that the receiving country or service provider upholds adequate data protection standards equivalent to those in Kenya. All cross-border transfers follow the guidance of the ODPC.

12. Age Restrictions

XbomaSwift is intended for users aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If such data is identified, it will be securely deleted.

13. Updates to This Policy

As XbomaSwift grows to serve more users and movers, this Privacy Policy may be updated. Any major changes will be communicated via your dashboard or official email notice.

14. Contact Us
