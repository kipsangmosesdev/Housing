XbomaSwift Help Guide

Everything you need to know about using XbomaSwift

For Tenants

Account Creation

  • Click the Login button at the top-right corner
  • Click Request Access
  • Select "I am a Tenant"
  • Fill in your details including:
    • Full name
    • Email address
    • Phone number
    • Preferred password
  • Submit the form

Logging In

  • Click the Login button
  • Enter your registered username/email and password
  • Click Login to access your dashboard
  • Forgot password? Click Reset Password link

Dashboard Overview

  • Buy Coins - Purchase KRH coins for property access
  • View Property Map - Interactive map of available properties
  • KRH Coin Balance - Your current coin amount
  • Your Profile - Update personal information
  • Favourite Properties - Saved properties
  • Recent Property Views - Your browsing history
  • Transactions - Payment and coin purchase records

Buying Coins

  • Click Buy Coins from the navbar
  • Select your preferred coin bundle:
    • 1 coin - KES 10
    • 5 coins - KES 45
    • 10 coins - KES 80
  • Enter your MPESA phone number
  • Confirm payment via MPESA prompt
  • Coins are credited immediately upon successful payment
1 coin = 1 property contact unlock

Viewing Property Map

  • Click View Property Map from dashboard
  • Use the search bar to locate specific areas
  • Click Locate Me to find your current position
  • Click on map pins to preview:
    • Monthly rent
    • Property images
    • Bedroom count
    • Exact location
  • Click the Directions icon to get travel distance and routes

Viewing Property Details

  • Click View Details on a map pin
  • See complete property information:
    • Apartment name and description
    • Amenities checklist
    • High-quality image gallery
    • Interactive map location
    • Landlord contact (requires coins)
  • Use Google Maps integration for directions
  • Save property to your favorites
  • Leave comments and view previous tenant feedback
  • Click Contact Landlord (deducts 1 coin)

For Landlords

Account Creation

  • Click Login then Request Access
  • Select "I am a Landlord" option
  • Fill in required details:
    • Full name
    • Email address
    • Phone number
    • ID/Registration document
  • Submit the registration form
Admins will review and approve your account within 24 hours

Logging In

  • Enter your registered email/username
  • Enter your password
  • Click Login button
  • Forgot password? Use Reset Password link

Dashboard Overview

  • Add/Manage Properties - Create and edit listings
  • View Property Map - See all your listings
  • Subscription & Receipts - Manage your plan
  • Analytics - Views, comments, and ratings
  • Performance Graphs - Engagement metrics
  • Occupancy Distribution - Location popularity
  • Tenant Inquiries - Contact history
Premium features like ratings analytics require Premium/Agent subscription

Adding Properties

  • From dashboard, click Add Property
  • Fill in property details:
    • Title and description
    • Property type (Apartment, House, etc.)
    • Exact location (address)
    • Monthly rent amount
    • Number of bedrooms/bathrooms
    • Amenities checklist
  • Pin the location on the map:
    • Use satellite view for accuracy
    • For new constructions, pin nearest landmark
  • Upload high-quality images (up to 8):
    • Include exterior shots
    • Show all bedrooms
    • Highlight amenities
  • Submit for admin approval
Go to My Properties to edit submissions. Approval takes up to 2 hours.

Subscription Plans

Plan Properties Price Features
Basic 1 property KES 599/month Standard listing
Standard Up to 3 KES 1,499/month Basic analytics
Premium/Agent Up to 7 KES 2,999/month Full analytics, priority placement
  • Payments are made via MPESA
  • Upon successful payment, your properties become visible
  • Receive email receipts for all transactions
  • Upgrade/downgrade anytime

Property Analytics

  • Total Views - How many times your listing was seen
  • Contact Clicks - Number of tenant inquiries
  • Ratings & Reviews - Tenant feedback (Premium only)
  • Performance Trends - Views over time
  • Occupancy Distribution - Popular locations
  • Comparison Metrics - How you rank in your area

Tenant Inquiries

  • View complete contact logs with timestamps
  • See tenant profiles (if available)
  • Filter inquiries by property
  • Mark inquiries as followed-up
  • Export contact lists for follow-up

Profile Management

  • Update your personal/company information
  • Add/change contact details
  • Write a professional bio
  • Upload verification documents:
    • ID copy
    • Business registration
    • Rental licenses
  • Change password
  • Set notification preferences

Landlord Tips

  • Keep listings updated - Remove rented units promptly
  • Quality images matter - Use good lighting and angles
  • Monitor subscription - Renew before expiry to avoid downtime
  • Engage with feedback - Respond professionally to reviews
  • Accurate pricing - Research local market rates
  • Detailed descriptions - Highlight unique features
