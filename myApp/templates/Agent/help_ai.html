Discover how to chat with our AI to find your perfect property quickly, easily, and intelligently.

How to Prompt the AI

You can speak naturally! Just describe what you want i.e. number of bedrooms, city, rent range, or amenities. The AI will understand and find properties that match.

What XbomaSwift AI Understands

  • Verification: Verified or Featured properties
  • Bedrooms: 1 to 5 Bedrooms
  • Bathrooms: 1 to 4 Bathrooms
  • Property Types: Studio, Bedsitter, Cottage, Villa, Townhouse, Maisonette, Apartment, Bungalow, Hostel, Condo, Double Room, Single Room
  • Location: City, Area, or Estate (e.g., Nairobi, Kilimani, Nakuru)
  • Amenities: Swimming Pool, Gym, Parking, Wi-Fi, Security, Balcony, Water,etc.
  • Rent Range: Below 10k, 15k to 20k, 20k to 50k, etc.

Sample Prompts

  • “Find me a 2 bedroom apartment in Nairobi with a budget of 20k.”
  • “Find verified and featured properties in Nakuru below 60k.”
  • “Show properties with a swimming pool and gym in Kilimani.”
  • “List all 3 bedroom bungalows in Eldoret below 50k.”
  • “Nionyeshe bedsitter huko Thika ya under 10k.”
  • “Tafuta 2 bedroom katika Nakuru yenye Wi-Fi na CCTV cameras.”
  • “Onyesha nyumba ambazo ziko verified karibu na Chuka University.”
  • “Tafuta apartment yenye parking na maji na rent ni below 20k.”

Tip: Be specific with city, budget, or amenities. XbomaSwift AI listens closely.
